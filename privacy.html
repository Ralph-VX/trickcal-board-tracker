Overview

Trickcal Board Tracker is a client-only web app for tracking character ownership and board upgrade progress. The app does not have its own backend server, account system, analytics service, or advertising system.

Data Stored in Your Browser

The app stores the following data in your browser's local storage:

  • Your tracker state, including owned characters and board upgrade selections.
  • Your selected language.
  • Whether Google Drive sync was enabled.
  • A local updated timestamp used to compare local data with Google Drive data.

This local data stays in your browser unless you export it, clear it, or choose to sync it with Google Drive.

Optional Google Drive Sync

Google Drive sync is optional. If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app requests access only to this Google Drive API scope: 

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This scope lets the app create, read, and update its own hidden app data file in your Google Drive appDataFolder. The app stores one JSON file containing your tracker state and sync timestamp.

The app cannot access, list, edit, or delete your normal Google Drive files.

Data Sharing

The maintainer does not receive your tracker data. The app does not sell, share, transfer, or use your data for advertising. If you use Google Drive sync, your tracker data is sent directly between your browser and Google's Drive API for storage in your own Google account.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete or revoke access to your data in these ways:

  • Use the app's Reset button to clear tracker progress in the current browser.
  • Clear this site's browser storage from your browser settings.
  • Revoke the app's Google access from your Google Account permissions page.
  • Remove the app's stored data from Google Drive app management, where available.
